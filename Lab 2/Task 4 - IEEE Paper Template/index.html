Detecting AI-Generated Images with a Fine-Tuned ResNet18

Affan
Department of Computer Science, Air University
Islamabad, Pakistan

Abstract—This paper describes a compact classifier that separates real photographs from AI-generated images. A ResNet18 network pre-trained on ImageNet is fine-tuned on the CIFAKE dataset. The report explains the data pipeline, training setup and evaluation approach.

Index Terms—deep learning, image classification, ResNet, synthetic images.

I. Introduction

Generative models now produce images that are hard to tell from real photographs. Automatic detectors help verify visual content. This work builds a small detector that can be trained on modest hardware.

II. Method

A. Dataset. CIFAKE [1] contains real and synthetic images labelled in two classes.

B. Model. The final layer of ResNet18 [2] is replaced by a two-class layer and the network is fine-tuned with a cross-entropy loss.
